Where Goa is

Goa decided to do this two weeks ago.

  • Goa AI Policy 2026 was released on 22 October at the Goa AI Tech Conclave by the Chief Minister and the IT Minister. A three-year framework, 2026 to 2029, covering every department, board, corporation and local body.
  • With it came the Goa AI Hackathon: 100 problem statements from 35 government departments, to be solved in phases with the Department of IT.
  • An AI Centre of Excellence with BITS Pilani Goa, and an innovation fund reported at ₹250 crore.

The policy belongs to the IT Department. The problems belong to departments like yours.

What already exists

A working twin of every local body in Goa. Live, not a proposal.

203local bodies: 189 village panchayats, 14 towns
770government schemes, read against each one
212Goa Online services tied to those schemes
1shared budget, voted on by local businesses

Each place carries its Census figures, its programmes, and a verdict on every scheme: eligible, plausible, or unlikely, with the reason and the next step. A separate side lets registered businesses put proposals on a public board, vote, and have a shared fund pay the vendor directly, every rupee on a public record.

Built and run by The Cybernetic Society. Schemes machine-read from goa.gov.in and the GIPARD handbook; not yet verified by any department.

What a twin is for

Try the decision on the model before spending on the real thing.

A twin is a model of a place that a government can run questions against. Singapore, Helsinki and Varanasi run city twins for exactly this; Kerala, Pune and Porto Alegre pair budgets with public votes. Questions your department could put to a Goa twin:

  • Which panchayats have the most households that look eligible for Dayanand Social Security but are not drawing it?
  • Where is the nearest day-care centre or physiotherapy centre for persons with disabilities more than an hour away, and what would one more cost?
  • Where does drinking-water coverage lag household growth since the last Census?

Our write-up of the real twins and participatory budgets, with sources: What is a digital twin, and why pair it with a shared budget?
