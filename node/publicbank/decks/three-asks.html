How it works

Shops say what they need. The AI writes it up. Shops vote. The kitty pays.

This is the board this morning. A shop signs up on its phone and says what it needs. When two shops need the same kind of thing, the AI writes it up as one proposal, puts a Goa price on it, and notes which government scheme could pay part of it.

The thumb is how many shops want it. Nothing gets a vote until one shop says "I'll run this" and another says "count me in". The kitty pays the printer or the trainer directly. No cash goes to a shop.

For youNo file, no clerk, no counter. A small sum, fixed dates, and anyone in the taluka can open the page and see where the money went.

Who did this before

Other places let people vote on the money. Here is what happened.

For youEvery one of these has a politician's name on it, and people still say the name. None of them started bigger than a taluka.
  • 🇧🇷Porto Alegre, 1989: the neighbourhoods queued up the city's building works themselves. Nine years later nearly every house had piped water and a sewer, up from three in four.
  • 🇮🇳Kerala, 1996: over a third of the state plan handed to 1,214 panchayats and towns to plan. Still the biggest thing of its kind India has done.
  • 🇮🇳Pune, 2006: ₹50 lakh a ward, from what residents asked for. Has run every year since.
  • 🇫🇷Paris, 2014: one rupee in twenty of the city's building money, voted project by project. Half a billion euros chosen by residents over one term.
  • 🇺🇸New York, 2015: each councillor put a slice of their money to a vote in their district. 67,000 people voted; 132 projects got built.
  • 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿Scotland, 2017: a rule that at least 1% of every council's budget goes to a public vote. Councils passed the mark by 2023.

What the studies found

Give people the vote on the money and three things happen.

The money goes to drains and clinics

Brazilian towns that let people vote spent more of the same budget on water, sanitation and health. Fewer babies died: one to three fewer per thousand born, a tenth to a fifth of the rate.

It gets better every year it runs

Twenty years of data from Brazil's big cities. The longer the vote had been running, the more it moved health spending and the more lives it saved.

Voters come back

New Yorkers who voted on a budget once were eight points more likely to vote in the next ordinary election. The biggest jump was among the young and the poor.

For youThe third one is the one that matters to an elected official. People who were handed a vote on money turn out again, and they remember who handed it to them.

What is the AI twin?

A ledger of every village in Goa that you can ask questions of.

Think of the old panchayat register, one page per village, with every government scheme written against it: this village can get this, that one cannot. Then imagine asking it a question and getting the answer before you send a jeep.

  • Singapore, Helsinki, Hamburg and Varanasi keep one of their whole city. Hamburg sat four hundred residents around it to decide where 20,000 refugees would live. Varanasi's won a national award.
  • Goa's is already written: all 203 panchayats and towns, with a yes, maybe or no against each of 770 schemes. Sanguem: 7 panchayats and a council, 9,041 households, 706 schemes within reach. /twin/taluka-sanguem
  • 66 of those schemes are yours. A machine read them from PDFs; nobody in your department has checked the reading yet.
For youEvery scheme you run, against every village you represent, on one page, before the next question in the Assembly about where it is not reaching.
