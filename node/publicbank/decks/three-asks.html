How it works, in thirty seconds

Register. Say what you need. Vote. The fund pays. The record is public.

  • A business registers on its phone in ten minutes: where it is, what it needs.
  • The AI twin reads every need, writes a draft when two or more rhyme, prices it for Goa, and names the government scheme that could carry part of it, drawn from 770 Goa schemes.
  • A draft reaches the ballot only when a business leads it and another backs it. Local businesses vote, one vote each, and a plan stands only if most of those who vote approve it.
  • The fund pays the vendor, never the business. Thirty days to the first payout, sixty to the second round, six months to the outcomes report.
For youNo file passes through a department. The sum is small, the dates are fixed, and the result is visible to every voter in the taluka who cares to look.

What the studies found

Money moves toward basics, and the people who vote keep voting.

Infant mortality fell 10 to 20%

Brazilian municipalities that adopted participatory budgeting put more of the same budget into sanitation and health. Gonçalves, World Development, 2014.

The longer it runs, the more it does

Twenty years of data on Brazil's largest cities: higher health spending, more civic organisations, lower infant mortality, stronger with each year. Touchton and Wampler, 2014.

Voters stay voters

New Yorkers who voted on a budget were about 8 points more likely to vote in ordinary elections afterwards, most of all the young and the poor. Political Behavior, 2023.

For youThe third result is the one for an elected official: people who have voted on money once turn out again, and they remember who gave them the vote.

All three are observational studies: association and dose, not a controlled trial. Our write-up with sources: What is a digital twin, and why pair it with a shared budget?

The twin

Singapore, Helsinki, Hamburg and Varanasi run one. Goa already has one, and nobody in government has put a name on it.

  • Singapore asks its island model how much a roof would earn from solar and how a bridge would move people. Helsinki published its twin as open data. Hamburg sat four hundred residents at a model of their districts to place housing for 20,000 refugees. Varanasi built a 160 square kilometre twin and won a SKOCH gold.
  • Goa's twin is of something smaller and more useful to a minister: every one of 203 local bodies, with its Census figures and a verdict on each of 770 schemes. Sanguem alone: 7 panchayats and a council, 9,041 households, 706 schemes eligible somewhere. /twin/taluka-sanguem
  • 66 of those schemes are yours: 39 Social Welfare, 27 for persons with disabilities. Machine-read, and not yet checked by anyone in your department.
For youEvery scheme you run, mapped to every village you represent, before the next question in the Assembly about where it is not reaching.

Three asks, in rising order

Each stands alone. Each makes the next easier.

One₹5 lakh for South Goa

The north has a fund from private money. Match it for Sanguem's businesses: same software, vote in 30 days, paid in 60, outcomes in 6 months.

We need: ₹5 lakh and a line from your office inviting the taluka's businesses.

TwoOne problem on the twin

Households that look eligible for Dayanand Social Security but are not drawing it, or where the nearest day-care centre is over an hour away. A ranked map in two weeks, camps for the top ten places in 90 days.

We need: one named officer and the figures the department already keeps.

ThreeOne per cent of one line

Scotland's rule on one scheme line, such as assistance to Self Help Groups: allocated by the groups themselves through a binding vote, filed under the Goa AI Policy 2026 as a hackathon problem statement.

We need: the line, the sum and the rule.
